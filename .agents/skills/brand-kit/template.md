## Global Writing Rules

*Add guidelines you'd like to apply to all content. Use one rule per line.*

**No AI sentence constructions**
No negative parallelism ("it's not X, it's Y"). Rewrite as a direct positive statement.
Break the rule of three. Do not default to three-item lists or three stacked adjectives. Use two, four, or a full sentence.
No false ranges ("from startups to enterprises") unless a real spectrum exists.
Default to plain "is" and "has". Do not replace them with inflated verbs (serves as, boasts, features, represents) unless the stronger verb adds real meaning.
No em dashes. Use a comma, parentheses, or a period instead. Use en dashes for ranges. No inline-header bullets ("Performance: performance improved by...").

**No AI word choice**
Always cut on sight: delve, tapestry, realm, leverage, robust, pivotal, underscore, seamless, cutting-edge, testament to. Use the plain word.
Cut only when two or more appear in one paragraph: foster, navigate, empower, streamline, crucial, myriad.
Cut only when they saturate the piece: significant, innovative, effective, compelling. Replace with specifics.

**No fabricated or unsourced claims**
Name the source. No "studies show", "experts say", "research suggests" without a real, specific citation.
No fabricated stats, dates, or ranges.

**No AI structure and rhythm**
Each paragraph should depend on the one before it. If you can reorder paragraphs and the reader would not notice, you wrote a list, not an argument. Add a through-line.
Every paragraph advances the point. If more than 40% could be cut with no information lost, the prose is restating itself. Cut it.

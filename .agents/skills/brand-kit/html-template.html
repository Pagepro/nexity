These figures disagree across the site. Do not reuse them until the client confirms which one is right.

Foundations

What the company is, who it serves and what sets it apart, in plain words. A strong version names the category, the customer and the problem it solves, says what the company does not do, and backs every claim with a source.

Brand Voice & Tone

How the brand sounds, read from its own best writing, so that content written by people or by AI sounds like the company. A strong version quotes real lines, shows how the brand proves its claims, and names the words and habits to avoid.

Global Writing Rules

The rules every piece of content for this brand follows, one per line. The base rules stop the most common AI writing habits, and the client rules come from this site, specific enough that an editor can check a draft against them.

Audiences

Who the content is written for, and which product line each audience maps to. A strong audience names a role and a situation, the worry that makes them act, and what they need to see before they trust the company.

Product Lines

What the company sells, one block per product line. A strong block says what the product does and for whom, how it is positioned against the alternatives, and which customer fits it best, with every figure sourced.

Claims register

Every checkable factual claim on the public record, so contradictions surface before content repeats them. Statuses: corroborated (2+ independent sources agree) · single source · third-party (stated only off-site) · tension (figures that collide when cited together) · unverified.
